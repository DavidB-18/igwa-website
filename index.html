Our Purpose

Driven by Vision,
Guided by Mission

IWGA exists to raise emotionally strong, financially educated, purpose driven women who can create wealth and influence across generations.

Our Mission

To empower women globally through mindset transformation, entrepreneurship training, financial literacy, practical skills, leadership development, and wealth education that helps them become confident, independent and unstoppable.

Our Vision

To build a global movement of women who are emotionally strong, financially educated, economically independent, purpose driven, and equipped to create generational wealth, leadership influence and lasting impact in their communities.

Dr. Gennelle Rice-Thomas

Dr. Gennelle Rice-Thomas is a visionary Grant Readiness Architect and Legacy Wealth Strategist dedicated to empowering purpose-driven leaders, nonprofits, entrepreneurs, and underserved communities through strategic funding readiness, organizational development, and generational wealth education. As Founder and Global Executive Officer of RiceGlobalEnterprise, she specializes in designing sustainable frameworks that bridge the gap between funding access and long-term legacy preservation. Through her Grant Intake Workflow and Legacy Workbook Framework, Dr. Gennelle equips organizations and families with the tools, strategies, and operational infrastructure needed to secure capital, strengthen compliance, build sustainability, and preserve generational impact. Her work sits at the intersection of grant readiness, economic empowerment, and legacy wealth-building helping visionaries move from survival mode into strategic stewardship. With expertise spanning Grant Administration, Organizational Capacity Building, Quality Systems, Financial Literacy, and Community Empowerment, Dr. Gennelle combines corporate precision with servant leadership to create transformational outcomes rooted in purpose, sustainability, and equity.

Mrs. Olubunmi Olufisayo Olashore

"The Liberator" | CEO, Liberated Resources | Lead Strategist

Olubunmi Olufisayo Olashore, widely known as The Liberator, is a transformational life coach, emotional intelligence consultant, real estate business strategist, speaker, and wealth empowerment advocate committed to helping individuals, women, families, and investors break free from limiting beliefs and build purposeful, profitable, and legacy-driven lives. She is the CEO of Liberated Resources, a transformational platform focused on mindset renewal, emotional intelligence, leadership development, personal growth, wealth education, and purpose activation. Through Liberated Resources, Olubunmi empowers individuals to rise above fear, self-doubt, financial limitations, and societal conditioning so they can discover their voice, unlock their gifts, and step confidently into leadership, influence, and sustainable success.
